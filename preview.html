About Jerry Wayne Brown

Editor, Curator & Family Keeper of the Flame

Jerry Wayne Brown is a Louisiana native whose deep love for family and literature led him to undertake one of the most meaningful projects of his life: preserving and presenting the written legacy of his brother, Bernard Anthony Brown — known to all who loved him as Benny.

"These words were already alive. My job was simply to make sure the world could hear them."

— Jerry Wayne Brown

With a meticulous eye for language and an unwavering commitment to honoring Benny's voice, Jerry undertook the initial editing and arrangement of 64 poems and short stories that Bernard had written over the course of his life. Drawing on the family's memories, manuscripts, and the unmistakable spirit of a man deeply rooted in his faith, his community, and the Louisiana he called home, Jerry shaped these writings into the book you hold today.

Into the Arms of Yahweh is his gift to every reader who has ever searched for God in the middle of grief, or found beauty in the familiar rhythms of the South.

A Tribute to Bernard "Benny" Brown

December 1955 — A life fully lived, a voice forever present

Bernard Anthony Brown grew up steeped in the rich cultural soil of Louisiana, carrying within him a love of language that would eventually fill pages with poems, parables, and stories. A graduate of Southern University with a degree in English (1984), Benny possessed a poet's ear for the rhythms of everyday speech and a preacher's instinct for truth.

"Let these words find you
wherever you are hiding —
in grief, in joy, in the long
quiet space between."

His writings range across the full spectrum of human experience: from devotional verses addressed directly to Yahweh and Yahshua, to sharp social commentary, tender love poems, and vivid short stories rooted in the Louisiana landscape he never stopped calling home. Through Jerry's patient editorship, every one of those voices has been preserved exactly as Benny left them — honest, unpolished in the best possible sense, and deeply, unmistakably alive.

This book is compiled with love, in his memory, by his family.
